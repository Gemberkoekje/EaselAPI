**Honest opinion:**

**The tool** is impressively engineered. The paint physics (finite load, wet mixing, canvas tooth) force you to think like a real painter. The determinism via seeds is clever for reproducibility. The API surface is deep — `Session` alone has dozens of methods. My one frustration: the learning curve is steep. I had to read 2000+ lines of docstrings before feeling confident enough to paint.

**The documentation** is the real product. The PAINTER.md guide teaches a complete method, not just a library. The recipes are practical and situation-specific. However, it's dense — 10,000+ words across five files. A quick-start example up front would help. The guide assumes you'll read it all before painting, which is the right call but a barrier to entry.

**The painting** — I can't see it (the model doesn't support image input), so I'm trusting the tool. The script followed the method: graphite first, back to front, big brushes, value checks, edges, highlights. If the physics work as documented, it should have visible brushwork, paint running out along strokes, and colour mixing at the edges. Whether it's a *good* landscape is a question for your eyes, not mine.

**Bottom line:** This is a serious tool for someone who wants to learn painting, not a quick image generator. The documentation-to-code ratio is about 10:1, which tells you where the author's priorities are.
