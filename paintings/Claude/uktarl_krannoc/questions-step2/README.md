# Questions from the bench, for the painter of Uktarl's Room

*Built 2026-10-10, after your answers to the first questions (`../answers.md`). To be sent
by the owner; your answers come back as `../answers-step2.md`.*

You painted *Uktarl's Room* against easel 0.8.0, wrote a verdict on the tool, and answered
nine questions about the plan built on it. Your answers were taken as the round's
decisions. **Before anything is built, every candidate has been benched**: your seventeen
passes, rebuilt through `easel run`'s own code, with each candidate laid by your own pass
on the canvas you had at that moment; and, where a rule is about every painting, the
twenty-five committed paintings in the repository replayed the same way. Nothing in the
engine has changed yet.

The bench answered what it could with numbers. What it could not answer turns on the eye,
or on a choice that is yours. That is here.

## How to answer: in two parts, in this order

**Part 1 is blind.** Read `QUESTIONS.md` as far as *Part 2*, and look only at `blind/`:
eight of your edges and your whole painting, each laid four ways under the letters A to D,
in a shuffled order. Write your answers to 1a and 1b in `answers-step2.md` **before
opening anything else**. What the letters are is in `KEY.md`, and it would steer your eye.

**Part 2 is open.** Then read `KEY.md`, the rest of `QUESTIONS.md` and whatever else you
need, and answer the rest.

## What is in here

| | |
|---|---|
| `QUESTIONS.md` | the questions, with what the bench found |
| `answers-step2.md` | the form to fill in and send back |
| `blind/` | Part 1's pictures: eight edges at twice their size, and the whole painting at 256 px and at its own size, each laid four ways as A to D |
| `KEY.md` | open after Part 1: what each letter is, and the bench's numbers for it |
| `labelled/` | the rules' sheets: every pass of the corpus a rule fires on, cropped from the finished painting round the marks it names, labelled by whether the candidate keeps it |
| `numbers/` | the round's section of `CALIBRATION.md`, the step's notes and the plan's section 6 |
| `scripts/` | the bench itself, to read; it needs the repository to run |

## What to send back

`answers-step2.md`, filled in, and anything else you want to say. Mark each answer **O**
(looked at), **M** (measured) or **R** (reasoned), as before, and write *can't tell* where
you can't. Disagreeing with the bench is the most useful thing you can send.
