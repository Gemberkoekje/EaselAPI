### 9a. Which reads as a form turning in stone?

Ranked, most like carved stone to least: **F; then B and C, tied; D; G; A; E.** The order

- **B and C**: I cannot tell them apart (**O**). By pixels they are almost the same one

- **D**: soft, and in places it turns (the back). But it reads as wet paint dragged, not

  far (**R**).

  arch were each a pass script, and each rehearsal cost a render of 85 to 113 marks to see

    laying paint.
  - Each mass call's place is drawn flat at its colour's value, clipped as the call clips,

- **Clip as part of a place: yes** (**R**). My committed lighting is three copies of the body,

    `smooth` and the rest, but no intersection).
  - So the dict needs a way to say it. A pair as the value, `{rim_in: ("mid_c", body)}`,

### D. The marks that landed nothing

**Yes: name them after the pass, only when the pass laid one.** Say which marks, at which
lines, and the cause the engine can see. (**R**)

- **The dearest line is not enough** (**O**). It lists only calls that cost more than one
  stroke, so a mark laid by hand never appears on it. Every mark that carried a picture's
  reason and landed nothing was laid by hand:
  - my spark (`p06_finish.py:39`, and my ember beside it landed 1.6 units);
  - the second painting's eight flour strokes, in a picture whose why names her floury
    hands.
- **`easel log` is not enough either** (**M**, **O**). Neither painter ran it while painting.
  - My 27 saved reports mention no mark that landed nothing, and neither do the second
    painting's 32 (its `answers.md`).
  - Its painter looked at the flour pass's rehearsal and wrote *the flour is faint but
    there*.
  - A command you have to remember is the one you do not run.
- **It will not become the next line nobody reads** (**R**). It speaks only when a mark
  literally did nothing, and no painter lays a mark meaning it to land nothing. *No clear
  light* was true and meant; this line is never meant. At 9.7% of passes (the bench's
  figure), it will speak about one pass in ten, and every time about something to act on.
- **One line, hand marks by name and mass calls by count** (**R**), e.g. *landed nothing: the
  dab at p06_finish.py:39 (a round tip under its cliff at press=1); 6 passes of the block_in
  at p05_details.py:12 (outside its clip)*.
- **The guide block it would catch counts for it, not against it** (**R**). *A mass built of
  planes*'s dry-brush stroke laying nothing at the check's canvas is a recipe bug, and the
  line would have found it.
- **On my ember: `dab()` does not say it** (**O**).
  - 0.7.0's `dab()` and the repository's at `da02328` both say one touch lands about a
    quarter of the way and three press through. Neither says what two touches do.
  - My ember at `press=2` read a tenth (the bench), below the one-touch figure the docstring
    gives. So the docstring leads a painter to expect *more* from two touches than from one.
  - The sentence that would have saved it, *`press=1` and `press=2` are whispers*, is in *A
    small round thing*, which I did not read. The recipe I did read, *A small irregular
    bright mark* (lines 623 to 666), uses `press=3` in its example, and I used `press=2` with
    its `tip_wobble=0.7` anyway (**O**, `p05_details.py:50`).
  - So the ember is my error with a gap in the docs beside it. Put the two-touch figure in
    `dab()`'s docstring, next to the one-touch figure.
