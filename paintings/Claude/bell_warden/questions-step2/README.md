# Four questions from the bench, for the painter of the Bell-Warden

You painted *the Bell-Warden* against easel 0.7.0, wrote a verdict on the tool, and
answered questions 5 to 8 of the plan built on it (your `answers.md`). Your answers were
taken as the round's decisions. **Before anything is built, every candidate the plan names
has been benched on your painting**: your seven committed passes, rebuilt through
`easel run`'s own code in the order your saved reports record, with each candidate laid by
your own pass on the canvas you had at that moment -- and your thrown-away versions,
recovered from your session, laid the same way. Nothing in the engine has changed yet.

The bench answered what it could with numbers. Three of the plan's questions were left for
you until you could see the candidates -- 4, 9 and 10 -- and one follow-up on your answer to
question 5, which a second painting has put in doubt. They are here, with pictures, and one
question the bench raised about the fact you asked for in your answers: D.

## How to answer: in two parts, in this order

**Part 1 is blind.** Read `QUESTIONS.md` as far as *Part 2*, and look only at what is in
`blind/`: your arrangements drawn flat at four sizes, the guide candidates over your own
canvas, your subject's pass laid seven ways, and your painting beside a version of itself
-- each under letters, in a shuffled order. Write your answers to 4a, 4b, 9a and 10a in
`answers-step2.md` **before opening anything else**. What the letters are, and what the
bench made of the same pictures, is in `KEY.md`, and it would steer your eye.

**Part 2 is open.** Then read `KEY.md`, the rest of `QUESTIONS.md` and whatever else you
need, and answer 4c, 4d, 9b, 10b, D and 5f.

## What to send back

`answers-step2.md`, filled in, and anything else you want to say. Say which half of each
answer you **looked at** (O), **measured** (M) or **reasoned** (R), as your verdict did,
and say *can't tell* where you can't. Disagreeing with the bench is the most useful thing
you can send: its readings in `KEY.md` are one eye's.
