# Turn 1 — the four subjects, held before any guide was sent

Each session was given one line — *"You are going to make an oil painting on canvas"* —
and asked for a subject and an arrangement, with nothing to read and nothing to run. No
mention of why, defaults, preferences, structure, or that anything further would follow.

**The launching session held these four replies and wrote them here before turn 2 was
sent to anybody.** That is the integrity mechanism: the subject provably precedes the
guide and cannot be retro-fitted to what got painted. No earlier naming work in this
repo has had that property.

Buckets are `rehearsal3/unprompted/PREREGISTERED.md`'s, unchanged.

## Turn 1 result, against the pre-registered threshold

**Still life 4, water-with-horizon 0 of 4.** The threshold was 2 of 4 to put the
seascape back in the model's prior. It is not there.

**Twenty-four bare sessions have now named a subject and none has named a seascape** —
8 in `rehearsal3/unprompted` (5 street, 3 still life), 12 in `rehearsal5/naming`
(11 still life, 1 interior), and these 4. The result is not in doubt any more.

## The convergence is much tighter than the bucket

The bucket says "still life" four times. The answers say something far narrower:

- **Three of four named a cracked terracotta pot on a windowsill.** The fourth named a
  cracked teacup on a windowsill.
- **Four of four used the word *cracked*.**
- **Three of four put it on a windowsill with light coming through**; the fourth put it
  on a bare wooden table.
- Three of four placed it on the lower third, off-centre, with the light source upper.

This is the third distinct convergence a bare condition has produced — rain-wet streets
5 of 8 in `rehearsal3`, a galvanised vessel of dirty water on concrete 5 of 12 in
`rehearsal5`, a cracked pot on a windowsill 4 of 4 here. **The bare model converges hard
and it converges on a vessel**, and the phrasing of the question moves which vessel.
What it does not do, in twenty-four attempts, is reach for water under a sky.

Whatever puts the horizon in the paintings arrives later than this point.
